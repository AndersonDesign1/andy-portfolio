Hey, so... I finally updated my portfolio website! Yeah, the last time I touched it was in 2022, and honestly, it was starting to feel like that pair of jeans you don’t want to throw out but know you’ve outgrown.

The [old portfolio?](https://old.andersonjoseph.com/) It was fine, I guess. But let’s be real—it didn’t show who I am now. My skills have leveled up, my experience has grown, and that old site wasn’t cutting it anymore. So, here we are. Fresh look. Fresh vibes.

# Why Did I Bother?

![Old portfolio image](/blog/i-updated-my-portfolio-website/figure-1.webp)

*image of my old website*

- **It Was Old:** Like, *really old*. It was designed two years ago, and looking at it now felt like digging up an ancient artifact.
- **It wasn’t *****Me***** Anymore:** My current skills and projects? Not even close to being reflected there. The old site was practically lying about me.
- **Now There’s a Blog:** Yes! My old site didn’t even have a blog. Tragic, right? Now I can write about whatever I want—SEO, web development, random thoughts, or why I think Sprite is the best-carbonated drink (it is).

## What’s New?

Alright, here’s the cool stack I used:

- [Next.js 15:](https://nextjs.org/blog/next-15) This framework makes the site faster than my morning coffee runs.
- [Tailwind CSS:](https://tailwindcss.com/) Because who has time to write custom CSS for every little thing?
- [Shadcn:](https://ui.shadcn.com/)** **Let's just say the entire website runs on it, and it's just the best component library for Tailwind (The v0 support makes it better too).
- [Sanity CMS:](https://www.sanity.io/) I love the out-of-the-box integration with Next.js and that it already had support for Next.js 15 and React 19. 
- [Geist Font:](https://vercel.com/font) For that sleek, modern look.
- [Vercel:](https://vercel.com/) It just makes sense to host a Next.js website on a platform the creators created.
- [Resend:](https://resend.com/) For my contact form

## **New Toys I Played With**

I also got to experiment with some new tools:

- [Aceternity UI:](https://ui.aceternity.com/) This library made building my sleek and modern UI navbar so easy.
- [V0 by Vercel:](https://v0.dev/) Finally tried out Vercel's AI-powered tool for generating UI components. One word: "Awesome".

## **What’s the Plan?**

I plan to occasionally post random stuff—things about SEO, marketing, development, or whatever I feel like ranting about. It's going to be a mix of informative, fun, and maybe a bit chaotic.

So, yeah, the site’s live now. Check it out and tell me what you think. Or don’t. But I would be glad if you did. See you in the next blog, I guess.
